## Import and duplicate handling
Original and reformatted catalogues overlap. Fourteen distinct source records were extracted, not two catalogues imported as separate inventories. Twelve representative door/frame records are rendered in the preview; an ancillary cabinet is shown separately and another ABS model is retained in the extraction. This is a representative reviewed slice, not a claim of a complete SKU migration.

Every record includes exact source name/page/label, crop coordinates, output dimensions and closed publication gates. See source-products.json and assets-provenance.md. Cross-check every label and supported variant with the product owner before changing verification status.
